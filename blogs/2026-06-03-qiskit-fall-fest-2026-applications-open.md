IBM has opened applications for the Qiskit Fall Fest 2026, the annual global community event that brings together quantum computing enthusiasts, students, and researchers. The Qiskit Fall Fest program enables universities and organizations to host local quantum computing workshops and hackathons using Qiskit, IBM's open-source quantum SDK.
