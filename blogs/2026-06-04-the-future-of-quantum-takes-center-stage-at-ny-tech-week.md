IBM hosted a panel discussion at New York Tech Week featuring quantum computing experts from major universities to explore how quantum processing units will become integrated partners with classical computing systems. The panelists highlighted emerging applications in molecular simulation and quantum networking, while emphasizing that algorithms developed by new researchers will be critical to advancing the field. Participants were encouraged to begin learning quantum computing immediately, as this represents a pivotal moment where AI and quantum discovery intersect.
